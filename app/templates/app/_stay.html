{# Moving around the app without ending the conversation. A normal link loads a new page, and a new page would close the call. So once the Assistant view is open, links and forms inside the app are fetched instead, and only the parts of the page that differ are replaced: the content, the language links, the page's own styles and the title. The conversation is never touched. Signing out and changing language still load a new page, and so does a session that ran out. #}
